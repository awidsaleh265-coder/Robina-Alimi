ABOUT ME

More than design.
It's communication.

I'm a creative and detail-oriented Graphic Designer passionate about visual communication and branding.

My interest in design began with drawing and gradually developed into digital graphic design. Today, I work on social media designs, posters, flyers, brochures, branding materials, presentations, ID cards and promotional content.

I believe design is not only about making something beautiful. It is about communicating a clear message and solving visual communication problems.
